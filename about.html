Founded In 1994

Whether you’re repairing your existing roof or installing a new one, getting the project done right the first time is essential. Founded in 1994, Newnan-based Roofing Enhancements, Inc. delivers solutions that are executed by a team of professionals with expertise across the full spectrum of residential, historic, and commercial buildings.

The end result of each project is the realization of the aesthetic vision of the customer, achieved through high-quality workmanship using name brand roofing materials for the specific features of the structure.

Our Mission

The mission of Roofing Enhancements, Inc. is to be the leader in low cost, superior-quality roofing services, backed by excellence in consistency and reliability when providing a service by a skilled tradesman who homeowners are proud to recommend to family and friends.
