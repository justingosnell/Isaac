Slate Roofing

Slate roofing tiles are one of the highest quality materials available on the market. These tiles give your home a unique look that conventional materials just can’t offer. Slate is also one of the most desired and longest lasting materials in the industry. A slate roof will give your home an elegant, one-of-a-kind beauty for over 100 years.


If you are considering using slate as your roofing material, there are a few things you should keep in mind. Slate roofing tiles can offer a variety of benefits, but there are some disadvantages as well. Let’s take a look at some of the pros and cons of using this unique natural stone as your new roofing.

What are the benefits of slate roofing

here are huge features of a slate roof that have made this one of the most desired materials on the market. Some of the more popular reasons for installing a slate roof include:.


Environment Friendly

Roof replacements generate more than 5% of the waste in landfills each year. Conventional roof materials need replacement every 20-30 years, and that adds up. Slate tiles, with proper care and maintenance, can last for 100 years or more!.

One-Of-A-Kind Beauty:

One of the biggest drawbacks to this material is the unique beauty of natural stone. Homeowners have more customization choices as well. Slate comes in a wide variety of options. You can choose from different sizes, colors and thicknesses to design that one of a kind look expressing your personal style..

Reliability:

With proper installation and care, your slate roof should last for 100 years or more. That’s huge compared to conventional materials with a 20-30 year life span

Fire Protection

Slate tiles are one of the most fire resistant materials on the planet. This is a considerable advantage if you live in a wildfire zone. But this material isn’t just for wildfire regions. Slate tiles can prevent fires caused by air borne sparks, like from fireworks or fires in adjacent homes.

Maintenance Free

Slate will not absorb water, and is highly resistant to temperature fluctuations. Inclement weather will not affect slate tiles, and won’t grow fungus or mold.
